============================================================
MODULE-LEVEL BREAKDOWN
============================================================

  Module                      Role                         Notes
  ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  gelu_top                Top-level passthrough         No logic, port connections only
  gelu_axi_stream_interface  AXI-Stream/Lite wrapper    FIFOs, counters, AXI-Lite CSRs (scalar; counts beats)
  8 × gelu_fp32               FP32 end-to-end datapath      generate g_lane[0..7], each a 12-stage chain
    fp32_to_q16               IEEE-754 → Q16.16             4 stages, barrel shifter + RNE
    compute_core              GELU PWL approximation        4 stages, 32×32 multiply  (per lane)
    q16_to_fp32               Q16.16 → IEEE-754             4 stages, CLZ + normalise + RNE

  Note: OpenLane flattens the hierarchy at synthesis; per-module area is not
  reported separately in the post-route stat. The 8,451 DFFs map to the 8 lanes
  × (12 pipeline stages × ~95 bits/stage in gelu_fp32) plus the scalar FIFO and
  counter state in gelu_axi_stream_interface (one handshake drives all lanes
  in lockstep, so control state does NOT scale with lane count).
