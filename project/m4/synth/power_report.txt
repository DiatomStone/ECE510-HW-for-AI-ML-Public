NOTES
============================================================

- Power estimation used OpenROAD report_power with post-route extracted
  parasitics (SPEF). Activity factors are the OpenROAD defaults
  (toggle rate = 0.1, static probability = 0.5).
- Combinational logic dominates at 84.3% of total power, consistent with the
  eight parallel 32×32-bit multiplies in compute_core stage 3.
- Switching power (51.9%) slightly exceeds internal power (48.1%): the design is
  net-switching dominated — typical for datapath-heavy arithmetic pipelines, and
  the 256-bit lane-packed buses add switching nets vs the 1-lane v1.
- For a tighter estimate, drive report_power with a VCD toggle-rate file from the
  cocotb x8 co-sim (tb_top, inloop_x8 target @ GELU_NUM_LANES=8) instead of the default
  activity factors. The current estimate overstates power for inputs near zero
  (PWL clamps early → low switching activity).
